THE ESSENTIALS

How it works

Choose an EMF or WMF file, inspect the rendered preview, then download an SVG, PNG or JPEG. EMF+ content inside EMF files is detected automatically. SVG preserves vector output; PNG supports transparency; JPEG uses a white background.

Supported formats & limitations

WMF, EMF and common EMF+ drawing records are supported. Certain legacy text, gradients, clipping and raster operations may be approximated or unsupported. Check the file diagnostics for details, or enable strict rendering to reject unsupported features.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. Your files are processed locally by WebAssembly. This site has no upload endpoint, account system or analytics.

Why might a conversion look different?

Windows Metafiles contain historical drawing commands. Some are approximated by the open-source renderer, and browser rasterization may differ from Windows GDI.

Is the converter free?

Yes. The converter is open source under Apache 2.0 and hosted as a static site.
